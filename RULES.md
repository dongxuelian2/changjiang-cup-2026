# Official information checked on 2026-11-01 (Asia/Shanghai)

Source: [official competition homepage](https://aiprotein.sjtu.edu.cn/), including
its published rules, schedule and Q&A, retrieved through web search's indexed
page. It identifies itself as an interim release.

- Each track accepts at most 20 distinct de novo sequences per team, with a methodology summary.
- Tracks concern target-specific binders, including VHH or minibinder formats; some may include scFv.
- Open-registration entries undergo structure/binding screening before selected candidates reach wet-lab testing. Verified seed-team entries proceed directly.
- A team's best experimentally measured candidate determines its ranking within a track.
- The homepage's current registration and sequence deadline is **2026-11-30**; it schedules target announcements for November 1.
- At retrieval, its challenge section still described target briefs, assay details, novelty checks and target-specific metrics as forthcoming. It advertised seven targets without identifying them in the accessible brief section.

These points agree with the user's stated rules. Older announcements use earlier
dates; this project follows the current official homepage. A precise deadline
time and time zone were not verified.

Access limitation: direct HTTPS retrieval failed with connection/transport
errors in both the browser and command-line attempts. The indexed official
homepage was readable, but its separate full-regulations link destination and
any newly released downloadable briefs could not be fetched. Consequently,
this is a verified summary of the accessible interim page, **not confirmation
